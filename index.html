About Naas Today

Hi! Naas Today was created to solve a simple problem: it's hard to know what's happening around town.

Events get posted across different Facebook pages, websites, and newsletters. This site brings them all together in one place, so you never miss what's on.

All events are submitted by the community and moderated before publication.
